Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

1. Information we collect

When you use Pet Nova we may collect the data you give us, such as:

  • Account details (name and email address).
  • Information about your pets (name, species, medical history and vaccines).
  • Location data, when you use Pet Map or Paw Alert.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: history, calendar, Paw Alert and Pet Map.
  • To send you reminders and important notices.
  • To improve Pet Nova and provide support.

3. Sharing information

We do not sell your data. We only share information when you authorize it, for example when you activate a Paw Alert, or when the law requires it.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

5. Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by writing to petnovitas2027@gmail.com.
